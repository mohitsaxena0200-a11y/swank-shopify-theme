# SWANK FASHION STORE — Design Contract

## Brand overview
SWANK sells fashion apparel, including men's winterwear. The intended audience is all-age fashion buyers. The experience should feel confident, premium, clear and welcoming.

## Design thesis
A restrained black canvas and precise gold accents frame fashion imagery like a luxury editorial, while shopping remains effortless.

## Colors
- Obsidian #111111 — surface-dark — hero, header and footer
- Soft black #191919 — surface-raised — dark panels
- Warm white #F7F6F2 — surface-light — primary page canvas
- Champagne gold #C6A15B — accent — rules, highlights and focus details
- Ink #171717 — on-light — primary text
- White #FFFFFF — on-dark — text on dark surfaces

## Typography
- Headings: existing Assistant font picker, weight 600–700, tight 1.05 line height.
- Body: Assistant, weight 400–500, 1.5 line height, comfortable measure.
- Display scale 2.5–5rem; section headings 1.8–2.6rem; body 1rem; labels 0.72rem uppercase with tracking.

## Spacing and composition
- Editorial wide margins; max content width 1440px; section rhythm 56–88px desktop and 36–52px mobile.
- Square or near-square corners; minimal shadows; strong contrast.

## Components
- Header: dark surface, white navigation, gold active/focus details.
- Primary button: champagne fill, near-black text, square corners.
- Secondary button: transparent with contrasting border.
- Product card: image-first, clear title and price.
- Collection grid: two columns mobile, four desktop.
- Footer: obsidian surface, white text, gold links/rules.
- Inputs: clear high-contrast border and focus ring.

## Dos and Don'ts
- Use actual product photography and clear prices.
- Keep navigation and add-to-cart obvious on mobile.
- Use gold as a measured accent, not a full-page fill.
- Avoid generic gradients, excessive badges, low-contrast text and invented claims.

## Motion philosophy
Subtle opacity and transform transitions only; respect reduced-motion preferences. No autoplay.
